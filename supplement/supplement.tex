\noindent This supplement gives the full model output behind the main paper. All models use the 583 listing-only chord sheets (symbolic analyses) or the 88 aligned recordings (audio analyses) described there. GEE models use a logit link, exchangeable working correlation and robust sandwich standard errors, clustered by song.
